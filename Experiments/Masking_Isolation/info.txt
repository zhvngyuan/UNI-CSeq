Masking-sensitive subset (racebench2.1)
=======================================

Source: Experiments/racebench2.1 (original multi-assert programs).
Layout: Experiments/Masking_Isolation/marking_sensitive_subset/<id>/<id>.c

Selection
---------
Among the 31 racebench2.1 programs, 12 use disable_isr/enable_isr outside
init()'s enable_isr(-1), which changes which ISRs may preempt a given region:

  001, 003, 004, 008, 013, 014, 019, 020, 026, 027, 028, 030

This subset keeps 10 of them. Excluded:

  svp_simple_004  ISR2 is masked but writes var2 with its existing value 0x22;
                  the var1 bug comes from never-masked ISR1.
  svp_simple_008  masked region writes/asserts array[40]; ISR writes array[0..9]
                  only, so masking does not change observable behaviour.
